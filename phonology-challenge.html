[d8]
Roll a d8. The number goes on the card.
20+[d80]

[d15*2]

[d12] [a/b/…]
Roll a d12 and take that option from the list.
[do/don't]
A list with no die. Two options flip a coin; more roll a die of that size.
(If do: …)
Only applies when an earlier roll came up “do”. Otherwise it is skipped.
[ipa:rare]
Deal up to 20 phonemes from a pool and roll for one. Pools: common, uncommon, rare, consonant, vowel, any place, manner or vowel height, front, back, rounded, unrounded, ejective, implosive, click, coarticulated, prenasalized, aspirated, nasalized, long and more. Join them with +, like [ipa:rare+vowel].

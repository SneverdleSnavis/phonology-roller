    Language profile

    What your deck commits the language to. Options that clash with it are crossed out before you roll.

    Phoneme cards use data from PHOIBLE 2.0 (Moran and McCloy 2019), licensed CC BY 4.0. Trimmed to single sounds found in 3 or more languages, with frequencies counted per language.

    Edit cards

    Paste rows straight from your roller spreadsheet, one card per row. Columns are separated by tabs (or |): category, card name, then the rule across as many columns as you like.

    [d8]
    Roll a d8. The number goes on the card.
    20+[d80]
    Roll a d80 and add 20. Negative numbers work too, like -1+[d9].
    [d15*2]
    Roll a d15 and double it.
    [d12] [a/b/…]
    Roll a d12 and take that option from the list.
    [do/don't]
    A list with no die. Two options flip a coin; more roll a die of that size.
    (If do: …)
    Only applies when an earlier roll came up “do”. Otherwise it is skipped.
    @place a/b/c
    A named list on its own row, anywhere in the text. Cards use it as [@place], like [d12] [@place].
    ejectives {airstream}
    Marks what kind of feature an option is, for lists that mix kinds. The card shows only “ejectives”.
    [ipa:rare]
    Deal up to 20 phonemes from a pool and roll for one. Pools: common, uncommon, rare, consonant, vowel, any place, manner or vowel height, front, back, rounded, unrounded, ejective, implosive, click, coarticulated, prenasalized, aspirated, nasalized, long and more. Join them with +, like [ipa:rare+vowel].
    family=…; needs=…
    An optional last column of tags, separated by semicolons. family allows one card of the family in the deck. needs=tone keeps a card out of the pile until the deck has tone.
    effect=include
    How a card writes to the language profile: include (or rule out on “don't”), exclude, neutralize, at-most, symmetric or count. It applies to the card's named list, or to feature=… for cards without one.
    counts=consonants
    The total a number roll sets, like consonants, vowels, moras or rare-phonemes. With effect=at-most it sets a ceiling instead.
